There are different realities. Noxeris is one of them, and so is yours - we call your reality Tricos. 

## Noxeris

Noxeris is the reality born from Areixyn's stories, the reality these stories exist in. 

Different stories exist in seperate universes. Universes are where most stories happen. Currently, there are two universes you can learn about: [Summermist Academy](/the-library/en/sma/sma) and [Project RE](/the-library/en/re/project-re).

The name Noxeris comes from the word root nox, the word reverie, and the Chinese word 實 (shi). Nox means night, and the night is one of Areixyn's representative items. Reverie means daydreams and 實 means real, and together they refer to how Noxeris is where reverie is reality. 

## Tricos

...Surely we don't need to explain your own reality to you?

Tricos is a nickname Areixyn gave the reality. (It would be weird to only call the reality you live in "reality" when all the realities are just as real to its residents, isn't that so?) The nickname comes from the word intricate, cosmos, and the Japanese word 虜 (toriko). Intricate comes from Areixyn finding Tricos to be a very intricate and complicated place. Cosmos is what the people of Tricos sometimes refers to the reality as. 虜 means prisoner or slave.
