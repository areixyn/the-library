**Final Bow** is a dance Nyx performed. 

An animatic of the performance can be found in this link: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCBwt7jOYxA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCBwt7jOYxA)

## Basic Information 

The song used is 少女A by Shiina Mota, and the choreography was done by Nyx. 

<Thumb
  src={finalBow}
  alt="A colored drawing of Nyx doing a curtsy in the animatic."
  caption="The thumbnail of the animatic."
/>

The performance takes place during sunset on a rooftop. There is a school desk by the railings at the center of the camera, and in front of the school desk is a school chair. 

Nyx has their hair in a half up ponytail, and has a purple flower hairclip on their bangs. Nyx wears an oversized translucent white shirt that is long sleeved and covers the top of their thighs. A black jacket is tied on their waist. They are wearing skin colored leotard and tights underneath. They wear black ballet flats. 

## Video Description / Choreography 

<Aside>
The description below uses numerous dance terminology. If you are unfamiliar with them, see the Reference Videos section below.
</Aside>

The video opens with a clapperboard. On the clapperboard, it is revealed that the title of the work is *Final Bow*, the music used is 少女A by 椎名もた, and the dancer / choreographer is Nyx Baili. 

Nyx puts down the clapperboard, walks to the chair and sits down, keeping their head down. 

 

intro: 

They look up. Hold arms out like a marionette. Hug their torso. Cover their eyes. 

Stand up slowly, then walk to the center and plie at 5th position. 

A sissonne, then a pique. 

A tombe pas de bourre, then glissade. 

A saut de chat, then a pique.

A tombe pas de bourre, a glissade, and then jump to 1st position. 

 

verse: 

僕の命っつったって | Reach left hand to side. 

誰の命っつったって | Reach right hand to side. 

時時々 公平に | Arms wobble but return to flat, like a balance scale. 

裁かれるもんなんでしょ | Arms tilt, right side down. 

暗い空にやってきた | Right hand sweeps in the air, feet in 2nd position. 

鬱を連れてやってきた | The hand twitches then come back down. 

時々雨 総計に | The hands alternately reach up then fall down like rain, feet tendu. 

頼り切りだ どうしよう | Two hands grab together, hold hands to chest, feet in 5th position. 

朽ちるまでの愛憎を | Hands and feet in 2nd position. 

朽ちるまでの愛憎を | Hands and feet in 5th position. 

飲み込む君 簡単に | Hands press down and plie. 

微笑む君 どうして | A tour en l'air. Hands shape a curve beside their face and smile. 

言葉を書く 曖昧に | A tombe pas de bourre to the left with hands spinning like pouring out something from chest. 

言葉を書く 曖昧に | Same as above, to the right side. The feet end in 4th position. 

伝わりきらんないから | They do a pirouette but fail and fall. 

君だけをさ 信じて | Hold hands to chest, then raise hands. 

 

pre chorus: 

捨ててきた夢をあつめて | Hands open and fall to side. Hands grab five times to the sides, gradually going up. 

ちょっと ちょっと | Hands alternately open to side. 

間違えたから | Hug the torso, arms forming a cross. Hands touch chest and shoulder, other side of chest and shoulder, then the waist. 

 

chorus: 

ああ 「時に 時に 躓いたって、」 | A sissonne, then two pique. Alternating hands grab and pull to the side. 

寒い寒い寒い寒い寒い | Alternating hands touch shoulders twice, chest twice, and the waist. 

寒い寒い寒い寒い寒い | Alternating hands touch butt, waist, jacket, thigh, and the inner thigh. 

寒い寒い寒い寒い | Alternating hands touch waist and chest. The hands end up choking the neck. 

言い寄らないで | Hands open, then plie. 

ああ 君の君の君の声が | A sissonne, sauté arabesque, pas de chat, tendu and move foot from front to side (a rond de jambe). 

遠い違い遠い遠い遠い | Chaine turns. 

遠い違い遠い遠い遠い | A tour en l'air, then the feet move to 4th position. 

遠い違い遠い遠い | A pirouette. 

傷つけないで | The pirouette fails again, and they fall to the ground. 

 

post chorus:

何番目でも 何番目でも | They stand up again mechanically. 

僕が僕であるために？ | They curtsy, step back, and reach forward with a pained expression. They lift their hand. 

 

verse: 

契り集め持ってきた | They get shoved to the side and stumble. They hold their hands to their chest and look at the direction where someone shoved them. 

契り集め持ってきた | They get shoved again, this time falling and crouching on the floor. 

あの日の間違いを | Their hands touch thigh and chest. They bury their face in their hands. 

飲み込むのが苦るしくて | They grab their hands together and pushes down while standing up. 

朽ちるまでの愛憎を | Hands and feet in 2nd position. 

朽ちるまでの愛憎を | Hands and feet in 5th position. They suddenly get slapped. 

飲み込む君 簡単に | They hold their face, then push down their hands. 

微笑む君 どうして | A tour en l'air. Hands shape a curve beside their face but no smile. 

 

pre chorus: 

「子供だましの花ふたつ、」 | Hands cover their eyes, then they do lotus hands in front of their eyes. 

きっと きっと | Hands reach to the sides like holding hands with someone. 

諦めたから？ | They happily pretend to have friends. They turn to see someone, then get in the leader's position for tango. 

 

chorus: 

ああ 遠い夢を追いかけてさ | A free spin dip with the dip substituted by a floating embrace. 

早い早い早い早い早い | They hug air, suddenly realizing that there's no one there. 

早い早い早い早い早い | They run around trying to find the person they were dancing with. 

早い早い早い早い | They keep running around. 

追いつけないよ | They find the person and runs to them happily. They get in the follower's position for tango. 

捨てきれず残した思いが | A free spin dip. 

憎い憎い憎い憎い憎い | They nearly fall, as if suddenly there's no one holding them up in the dip. 

憎い憎い憎い憎い憎い | They hold their head in pain, running around.

許されないの？ | They mouth the lyrics with a pained expression. They fall back onto the chair. 

 

ああ 夢を夢を見てたはずが | Their head turn with an empty expression. They raise their hand over their head, then put their hand back down. 

怖い怖い怖い怖い怖い | They hold their head in pain. 

怖い怖い怖い怖い怖い | They hold their head in pain, curling up. 

怖い怖い怖い怖い | They hold their head in pain, hugging a leg. 

近づかないで | They step up on the chair and reach out a hand. Their expression goes from scared to calm. 

合言葉を繰り返すだけ | They turn back, then step onto the table. Their hold out their arms and turn. 

寒い寒い寒い寒い寒い | Their hands hug their torso. 

寒い寒い寒い寒い寒い | Their hands trail towards their chest and and their thigh, and they slowly turn to face the camera again. 

寒い寒い寒い寒い | Their hands move to their shoulders and legs as they squat down. 

お願いだから | They kneel on the table and raise their hands. Their hands open to the side. 

 

post chorus:

何番目でも 何番目でも | They stand up on the table fluently. 

僕が僕であるために⋯ | They curtsy, step back onto the railing, and reach out while falling back with a smile. 

 

outro: 

A shot of the empty rooftop. 

Nyx is falling down. 

They close their eyes as the video fades to black.
