**Noxeris** is the reality where the universes that Areixyn creates exists. 

In Noxeris, there are different universes. Universes are where most stories happen. Currently, there are two universes you can learn about: [Summermist Academy](/the-library/the-library/en/sma/sma) and [Project RE](/the-library/the-library/en/re/project-re).

Areixyn and Metsys live outside of the universes, which is why they are called residents of nowhere. This library, along with the [Lobby](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/), both count as places in nowhere, too.
