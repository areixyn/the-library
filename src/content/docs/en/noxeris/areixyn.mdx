## Trivia

- Areixyn's greeting is "the specter of dreamful nights greets you with (noun), dear (name)", and their farewell is "may your dreams be starry and your reality be sugary".
- Areixyn sometimes refers to Metsys using the nickname Mets.[^2]
- Areixyn is implied to have multiple backstories.[^1]
  - Areixyn once lived in Tricos. 
  - Areixyn left Tricos on aer birthday.[^1]
- Areixyn has a dead child self called Poemu.[^2]
