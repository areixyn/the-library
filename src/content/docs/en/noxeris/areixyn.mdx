## Appearance

Areixyn does not have a specific normal outfit. They have long silver hair that reaches their waist, and the ends of their hair is purple. Ae has M-shaped bangs, the left of the bangs is a long, thin sideburn. They wear a hairclip that is made up of three violet flowers, a small section of fabric in the color of a night sky, and a bigger part of translucent purple yarn. The translucent yarn covers xyr left eye. Their eyes are purple, and the pupil of their left eye is a star. Aer left eye is drawn without highlights. They wear a accessory that looks like headphones; the headphones are dark purple and have a purple star or violet in the center. Connecting the two headphones is a white string that wraps around the back of aer head, and some stars hang from the string. 

## Trivia

- Areixyn's greeting is "the specter of dreamful nights greets you with (noun), dear (name)", and their farewell is "may your dreams be starry and your reality be sugary".
- Areixyn sometimes refers to [Metsys](/the-library/en/noxeris/metsys) using the nickname Mets.[^2]
- Areixyn is implied to have multiple backstories.[^1]
  - Areixyn once lived in [Tricos](/the-library/en/noxeris/reality). 
  - Areixyn left Tricos on aer birthday.[^1]
- Areixyn has a dead child self called Poemu.[^2]
