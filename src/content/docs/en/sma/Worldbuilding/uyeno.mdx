**Uyeno** is an international organization founded by sorcerers in Summermist Academy. The organization aims to provide a place for sorcerers to communicate with each other and practice magic. Uyeno, the name of the organization, also means knot in the language of magic.[^2]

Uyeno has many branches, but for safety purposes members of a branch rarely know about other branches. To recognize other branch members, they use a code that gets changed at random intervals to ensure secrecy.[^1]


## Knot For Bookworms

Knot For Bookworms is a book club on the surface, but actually one of Uyeno's many branches underneath. Currently, [Krishna](/en/sma/characters/krishna), [Arashi](/en/sma/characters/arashi), and [Ophelia](/en/sma/characters/ophelia) are all members of the book club. 

There are about ten members in the club, and their ages vary.[^1] Members have weekly meetings held in the members' homes. They split into groups depending on the type of magic they wield and then practice magic together.[^2] When practicing magic, they use a trace suppression barrier to let the sorcerers practice magic without needing to worry about the traces of magic. 

When joining the book club, the leader asks the newcomer some questions, including what type of magic they use and their Shasuti name. The newcomer will then need to cast a spell, as a way to check whether they really are a sorcerer. After they pass the check, they get told the identification code and will then be assigned to the group of their magic type.[^1]
