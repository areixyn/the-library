Magic exists in the universe of Summermist Academy. There are different types of elemental magic, and there are sorcerers, who can use magic, and perceivers, who cannot use magic but can sense traces of magic. 

Below are some terms that might apply to both sorcerers and perceivers. For more information on either, see [Sorcerer](/en/sma/worldbuilding/sorcerer), [Perceiver](/en/sma/worldbuilding/perceiver), or [Uyeno](/en/sma/worldbuilding/uyeno).


## Traces of Magic

Traces of magic are left behind every time a sorcerer uses magic. To perceivers, traces of magic include information about the type of magic used, the strength of the spell, and the position of the user relative to the perceiver.[^1] However, sorcerers can only discern the strength of the spell, and are unable to distinguish between types or know where the trace is from.[^4]

Different sorcerers or perceivers experience traces of magic differently. 


## Price of Magic

The price of magic is a negative side effect of having magic.[^2] It can be mental, such as affecting language or emotions, or it can be physical, such as episodes of pain. The prices of magic don't necessarily follow rules of biology or science. 

The better someone gets at magic, the more severe the price becomes. The price of magic for spirit sorcerers seems to be higher than other types. It is currently unknown whether perceivers also get affected by the price of magic.


## Gods

There is little known about the gods, but we do know that they exist. 

The god of spirit magic is called Inowa Si.[^4] This is currently the only name we know of. 

In the childhood of sorcerers and perceivers, they get a dream of a god telling them about their powers, including their type of magic and their price.[^3][^4]

There are legends that gods gave sorcerers their magic.[^1]
