**Sorcerers** are people who can use magic in Summermist Academy. Sorcerers are also called wizards, which is a derogatory term. The public seems to view sorcerers as dangerous, with news stories sometimes covering incidents that sorcerers caused.[^1][^6]

Sorcerers are able to use magic from birth, but practicing can refine their skills and allow them to learn more advanced spells.[^2] There are four tiers of sorcerers: beginner, intermediate, advanced, and master.[^5] Sorcerers can also cast spells in different ways depending on their familiarity with the spell. For a spell that they are unfamiliar with, they can only use writing, such as using a finger to write in the air.[^6] The highest form of casting spells is chanting the spell in the mind.[^4]

Sorcerers have an amount of mana, which affects how many spells they can cast without resting. As a sorcerer becomes more experienced, they will have more mana and thus can cast more spells or stronger spells. Stronger spells will take more mana. Spirit spells also take more mana on average.[^4] If a sorcerer is low on mana, overuse symptoms start appearing. 

Sorcerers seem to have some sort of born ability related to their respective type. Currently, we only know that water sorcerers can hold their breath longer in water.[^6]


## Types of magic

There are five types of magic: water, fire, wind, earth, and spirit. The public does not know about spirit magic, and thus think that there are only four types. Even among sorcerers, the existence of spirit magic does not seem to be widespread.

Different types of magic seem to be associated with different colors, as seen in the different colors of markings of magic. Water type is blue, fire type is red, wind type is yellow, earth type is green, and spirit type is purple. 


## Markings of Magic

A marking of magic is a symbol on a sorcerer's body.[^3] The marking looks like a hash symbol with two long spirals coming out of the top and bottom. The marking has a different color depending on the type of sorcerer. Water type is blue, fire type is red, wind type is yellow, and spirit type is purple. The marking can be anywhere on the body, except for spirit sorcerers who always have it in one of their eyes, making the eye purple (dull usually, bright when using magic). 

Most sorcerers use a marking concealment spell to make the marking invisible. When using magic, the marking will appear and glow.

## Spells

Spells have different levels. As a sorcerer becomes more powerful, they will learn about and can cast higher level spells. 

Aside from spells of different types of magic, there are also common spells, which can be used by any type of sorcerer. 

### Marking concealment spell

A marking concealment spell can conceal the marking of magic on a sorcerer. It is commonly used with an enchantment spell so that the spell lasts longer.[^3]

### Enchantment spell 

An enchantment spell can enchant an object with a spell, allowing the spell to continue working until the mana runs out. It has to be used with another spell. 

<Thumb
  src={magicEnchant}
  alt="An image of the marking of an enchantment spell."
  caption="This is the marking of an enchantment spell on Krishna's hearing aid."
/>

Enchantment spells create a marking on the enchanted object. The marking looks like a hash symbol with a spiral inside. The marking glows when the spell is working, and stops glowing when it has run out of mana. A sorcerer can recharge the mana through the marking without recasting the entire spell.[^3]

### Trace suppression spell

Trace suppression spells can suppress the traces of a spell that is casted. It has to be used with another spell. 

### Trace suppression barrier spell

A trace suppression barrier is a barrier where traces of magic in the area will not be detected outside the barrier. 

### Nullification spell

A nullification spell nullifies any spell in a certain area, making it impossible to cast spells. The nullification spell is a high level spell, meaning that only advanced sorcerers learn about and can cast this spell.
