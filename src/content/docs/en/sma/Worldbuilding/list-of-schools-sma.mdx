Other than Summermist Academy, there are a couple other schools in the universe. This is a list of the other schools. 

## Junior High Schools

### Sakura Junior High

Characters that have enrolled in this school include [Krishna](/the-library/en/sma/characters/krishna), [Cyrus](/the-library/en/sma/characters/cyrus), [Samiel](/the-library/en/sma/characters/samiel), and [Aimee](/the-library/en/sma/characters/aimee). 

- Sakura Junior High has a dormitory. The rooms in the dorms are quad rooms.[^1]

### Snowflake Junior High

Characters that have enrolled in this school include [Arashi](/the-library/en/sma/characters/arashi), [Daisy](/the-library/en/sma/characters/daisy), and [Ophelia](/the-library/en/sma/characters/ophelia).

- Snowflake Junior High does not have a dormitory.[^2]

## Elementary Schools

### Milky Way Elementary School

Characters that have enrolled in this school include Krishna, Arashi, and Daisy. 

### Sunset Elementary School

Characters that have enrolled in this school include Ophelia, Cyrus, and Aimee. 

### Cloudtop Elementary School

Characters that have enrolled in this school include Cyrus and Samiel.
