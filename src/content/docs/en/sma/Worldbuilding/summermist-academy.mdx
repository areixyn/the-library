Summermist Academy is a high school in the universe Summermist Academy. 

## School Facilities 

- There is a dormitory. 
  - The dorms of the school feature double rooms. 
  - The rooms have separate rooms inside where the student's bed goes. 
  - The bathrooms are built inside the dorm rooms. 
  - There are normal rooms and accessible rooms. Normal rooms have bunk beds while accessible rooms don't[^2], and accessible rooms are also bigger[^6].
  - A sofa and a small table come with the normal dorm rooms. The students can decorate their rooms however they want. 
  - Microwaves are not allowed in the rooms. 
  - Living in the dorms is not required. 
- Summermist has a cafeteria. 
  - According to Arashi, the cafeteria food is good.[^3]
  - Some students also order delivery. 
- There is an outdoor basketball field and a student activity center.[^4]

## Uniform

<Thumb
  src={uniformImg}
  alt="The cast wearing uniforms."
  caption="The cast wearing uniforms."

- The school uniform is a purple sailor shirt with a black bottom. 
  - The shirt has a pocket on the left chest. 
  - The tie can be a tie of the student's choice as long as it is purple. Currently seen options include sailor ties, bow ties, string bow ties, short and regular neckties. 
  - Choices for bottoms include shorts, pants, or skirts. 
  - The uniform jacket is a black suit jacket with gold buttons and trims on the pockets. 
  - The uniform cardigan vest is purple with white trims. 
  - Student IDs are embroidered on the left chest above the pocket. 

<Thumb
  src={sportswearImg}
  alt="Cyrus wearing the school's sportswear."
  caption="Cyrus wearing the school's sportswear."

- The sportswear top is a white shirt and roughly a quarter from the bottom is purple. There is one thin wavy stripe of purple above the purple section. It also has purple trims. The bottom is purple shorts or pants.
  - The shirt has a pocket on the left chest.  
  - The sports jacket is a white jacket with two light purple stripes on the arms. 
- Other than uniforms, Summermist also has school souvenirs, including hoodies.[^7]
- Summermist does not check the students' attire for daily activities, meaning that they can wear whatever they want usually.[^7]

## Courses & Clubs

- There are many clubs in the school. Students go to a club of their choice during a club period, and can also choose to go to other clubs if those clubs have extra classes.[^2]
  - The club period is on Friday afternoons.[^5]
  - Clubs that have appeared include: 
    - Astronomy club
    - Spirits of the Air (poetry club)
    - Dessert club
    - Dancing club
    - Basketball team
- A third language is required for students. Current known options include Japanese, Korean, Spanish, and Russian.

## Location

- Summermist Academy is on a mountain called Mt. Lilihaze.[^1]
  - The mountain is often foggy, and there are also many lilies. 
- It's often foggy in Summermist Academy.[^1]
- There are at least two beverage shops near Summermist. One of them is more expensive, with the cheapest option being 70 dollars.[^3]

## Other

- The school's anniversary is on May 27th. Celebrations are usually held on the weekends of that week. 
- The school's signature color is purple.
