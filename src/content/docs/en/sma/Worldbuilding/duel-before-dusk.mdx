**Duel Before Dusk**, or DBD for short, is a fictional shooting game in Summermist Academy. 

## Game mechanics

- It is a first person shooter game. 
- There are currently two known modes in the game: Battle Royale mode and Duel Before Dawn. 
  - Battle Royale mode is like a classic battle royale game. Players find resources across the map and fight each other. However, the game encourages players to end the battle before sundown (6 o'clock) in game, which is also what the name of the game means. Players can look at the in game sky or find clocks in the map to tell the time.[^2]
  - There is a single player mode and a multiplayer mode. The multiplayer modes requires 4 or 5 players in a team to start the game.[^3] 
  - In Duel Before Dawn mode, the game takes place at night, making resources harder to find. The player also needs to pay attention to their body temperature as they can freeze to death. There are also “immortal people who are out to kill others.”[^2] 
- It has a lot of customization options for the player characters.[^1]
- When the game starts, scratching sounds of paper will play, and a piece of paper appears on the screen with the word signature written on it and a line underneath, like a signing area for a document. After the player writes something, the game starts. The player only gets one stroke.[^1]

## CL Clan and Team A Plus

There are many clans created by players in the game, and CL Clan is one of them. 

CL Clan's full name is Chameleon Clan. Its name symbolizes how the members can kill enemies efficiently and effectively like chameleons. Members of the clan have a unique format for their username, including a “Cl” at the front to signify their clan.[^2]

Team A Plus is one of the many teams in CL Clan. The team name can refer to how they are one of the best teams, but can also refer to how all the members have an A in their online name.[^2] Currently, the members of team A Plus include Nana ([Arashi](/en/sma/Characters/arashi)), Akaoni ([Cyrus](/en/sma/Characters/cyrus)), Ela ([Samiel](/en/sma/Characters/samiel)), Aqua, and Maple. Other than playing the game, the team sometimes also film videos for game strategies.[^4]

## Trivia

- Areixyn was not aware of Dead by Daylight when they created this fictional game. The similar acronym is a pure coincidence.
