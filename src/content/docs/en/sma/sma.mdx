**Summermist Academy** is a universe in Noxeris.

Friendships, magic, and maybe a bit of trauma — well, that's just the average high school experience, isn't it?

Join 7 highschoolers as they navigate relationships, past memories, and the average high school life.

## Trivia

- Summermist is mostly written by Areixyn, but Y and 17 are also co authors. 
  - Y and Areixyn work on most character designs and worldbuilding together. Areixyn has full control over Krishna, while Y has full control over Arashi. 17 works on everything about Samiel.[^1]
- The universe code for Summermist Academy is sma.
