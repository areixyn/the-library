**Samiel Stuart** (司徒蘇念) is a main character in [Summermist Academy](/the-library/en/sma/sma).

## Basic Information

- English Name: Samiel Stuart
- Chinese Name: 司徒蘇念
- Other Names: Ela (online name)
- Number: 23
- Birthday: 11.07 (Scorpio)
- Gender: male (he/him)
- Sexuality: heterosexual
- Height: 179
- Weight: average 
- Representative Color: red
- Representative Item: glass lock
- Club(s): Dancing Club
- Hobbies: dancing, gaming
- Fashion Style: streetwear
- Magic: [wind sorcerer](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/sorcerer)

## Appearance

Samiel has white hair tied in a high ponytail. The ponytail doesn't reach past his head. He has M-shaped bangs with long, thin sideburns. He wears two earrings shaped like red bars that reach the level of his chin. His eyes are light red. His skin is paler than most of the others.

In [uniform](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/summermist-academy/#uniform), Samiel wears the short sleeved uniform. His tie is a necktie. He wears a rose gold colored tie clip, one end shaped like a keyhole and attached to the top of the tie, the other end shaped like a lock that is attached to his shirt. He wears three rings, black ones on his ring finger of his right hand and index finger of his left, and a light red one on the middle finger of his right hand. He wears pants with two rose gold belt chains and one has a lock charm on it. He wears white sneakers.

Samiel's fashion style is streetwear. His casual outfit is a white T shirt and a black leather jacket with chains on it. A chain on his left shoulder has a light red lock attached to it. He wears a necklace with a silver ring attached to it. He wears the same rings as his school outfit. He wears black work pants with the same sneakers as his school outfit. 

## Past

### Elementary School 

- Samiel went to [Cloudtop Elementary School](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#cloudtop-elementary-school).
  - He was friends with [Cyrus](/the-library/en/sma/characters/cyrus), although the two were not in the same grade.[^7][^3]

### Junior High School 

- Samiel is one year older than the others. He was one grade ahead of the others at first, but at some point something happened related to his sickness, causing him to be in the same year as the others.[^3]
- Samiel went to [Sakura Junior High](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#sakura-junior-high) during grades 1 and 2. 
  - He was classmates with [Krishna](/the-library/en/sma/characters/krishna), Cyrus, and [Aimee](/the-library/en/sma/characters/aimee). He didn't live in the school's dorm.[^1]
- Something happened in grade 2, causing Samiel to transfer to another school.[^8]

## Trivia

- Samiel doesn't live in the school dorms, as he needs to take care of his younger brother. 
  - Cyrus commented that if he did, they would have been roommates.[^1]
- His third language is Spanish.
- He is the chief of discipline in class.
- He has a younger brother named Michael, younger than him by 4 years. His parents appear to be divorced or separated.[^6]
  - Michael uses a wheelchair.[^1]
  - Michael is in 1st grade of junior high school.[^6]
  - His father often works overtime.[^5]
- His [price of magic](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/magic-sma/#price-of-magic) is a condition called RDX-1107 or kanimaok. 
  - The condition causes him to have episodes where he experiences pain that feels like wind scraping against bones and eroding his insides. During episodes, he also experiences blurred vision, nausea, heightened sense of pain, and coughs blood.[^2]
  - The name kanimaok is in Shasuti, and it means erosion of bones. 
- His [marking of magic](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/sorcerer/#markings-of-magic) is on his right arm, and he conceals it using a ring that he wears as a necklace.[^4]
  - He also has a canary tattoo over the marking that is the same color as it. 
- Samiel is a member of Team A Plus in CL Clan.
  - He seems to have joined it later.[^9]
