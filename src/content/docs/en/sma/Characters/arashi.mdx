**Arashi Hanazawa** (花澤嵐) is a main character in [Summermist Academy](/the-library/en/sma/sma).

## Basic Information

- English Name: Arashi Hanazawa
- Chinese Name: 花澤嵐
- Other Names: Nana (online name), Lamis (shasuti)
- Number: 7
- Birthday: 09.16 (Virgo)
- Gender: female (she/her)
- Sexuality: bisexual
- Height: 160
- Weight: average
- Representative Color: green
- Representative Item: duck
- Club(s): ACG Club, Astronomy Club
- Hobbies: drawing, gaming
- Fashion Style: casual
- Magic: [water sorcerer](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/sorcerer)

## Appearance

Arashi has short black hair that reaches her shoulders with long sideburns that reach her chest. She has blunt bangs slightly parted at the right side, and also a long, curly ahoge. She wears rectangular green hair clips at the top of her sideburns, and a duck hair clip close to the bottom of her left sideburn. Her eyes are light green.

In [uniform](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/summermist-academy/#uniform), Arashi wears the sports jacket — a white jacket with two light purple stripes on the arms — over her uniform. She rolls the jacket's sleeves up. Her tie is a bow tie. She wears a green watch and a green bracelet with a duck charm on her left hand. She wears a skirt with black over the knee socks. She wears white sneakers.

Arashi's fashion style is casual. Her casual outfit is a hoodie, with the top half white and bottom half muted green. She wears a pair of light green headphones around her neck. She wears a pair of jean shorts that are covered under the hoodie. On her left leg, she wears a black thigh garter belt. She wears black above ankle socks and light green sneakers. 

## Past

### Elementary School 

- Arashi went to [Milky Way Elementary School](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#milky-way-elementary-school). 
  - She was classmates with [Daisy](/the-library/en/sma/characters/daisy) during grades 3 and 4. 
- In 3rd grade, Arashi told Daisy about her magic.[^8]

### Junior High School
 
- Arashi went to [Snowflake Junior High](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#snowflake-junior-high). 
  - She was classmates with Daisy during junior high school, though not during 3rd grade.[^9][^10]
  - She was classmates with [Ophelia](/the-library/en/sma/characters/ophelia) during 3rd grade.[^10]

### Other

- Arashi was once nearly caught being a sorcerer due to her price of magic.[^3]

## Trivia

- She lives in the same dorm room as Daisy. 
  - They've decorated their room with a cozy corner that features a rug, pillows, and bean bags.[^6]
  - They share a laundry basket.[^3]
- Her third language is Japanese.
- She is the class treasurer in the class.[^1]
- Her [price of magic](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/magic-sma/#price-of-magic) is that she starts speaking Shasuti when she suppresses her emotions too much.[^3]
- Her [marking of magic](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/sorcerer/#markings-of-magic) is on the back of her left wrist, and she conceals it using her watch.[^5]
- Arashi is a member of Team A Plus in CL Clan.
- Arashi is good at calculating and math.[^1]
- Arashi's gaming headset is white and light green with cat ears and a mic, although she doesn't use the mic and instead uses typing to communicate online. She says the reason for this is because some people can be emotional online.[^2]
- Arashi knows Japanese, although it's currently unclear how fluent she is.[^2]
- Arashi has two pet ducks named Milk Tea and Calpis.[^4]
