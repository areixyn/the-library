**Krishna Tinta** (墨零) is a main character in [Summermist Academy](/the-library/en/sma/sma). 

## Basic Information

<Spoiler>Krishna is known to have OSDD. Some of the information below may only be apply to Krishna's host alter, Space.</Spoiler>

- English Name: Krishna Tinta
- Chinese Name: 墨零
- Other Names: Sikota (shasuti)
- Number: 3
- Birthday: 12.31 (Capricorn)
- Gender: female (she/they)
- Sexuality: asexual
- Height: 156
- Weight: underweight
- Representative Color: black (sometimes also brown)
- Representative Item: bandaid
- Club(s): Astronomy Club, Spirits of the Air
- Hobbies: stargazing, studying, writing
- Dressing Style: minimalistic
- Magic: [spirit sorcerer](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/sorcerer)

## Appearance

Krishna has long hair that reaches her waist, in a dark brown color close to black. She has m-shaped bangs that are split in the middle, and she wears a light brown and white hair clip that's shaped like a cross with bandaids. Her left eye is black, and she wears an eyepatch on the right side. Her right eye is a dull purple with a marking of magic in the center. Her eyes are often drawn without highlights. Her skin is paler than most of the others.

## Past

### Elementary School

- Krishna knows [Ophelia](/the-library/en/sma/characters/ophelia) from a past event, although currently it is unknown what this event is. Ophelia seems to have forgotten this event. 
  - Krishna appears to know a lot about Ophelia's magic, including the fact that she is a sorcerer[^3] and her [price of magic](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/magic-sma/#price-of-magic)[^11]. 
  - The two had specific ways for comforting each other. Krishna would pat Ophelia's shoulder[^3], while Ophelia would hold Krishna's hands[^7].
  - In *Shattered Illusions*[^7], Ophelia has an impression that Krishna cannot speak during her implied panic attack / alter switch.
  - In *First Meeting*[^10], Krishna recognizes and calls out Ophelia's name when they first meet in high school, but Ophelia does not recognize her. 
- Krishna went to [Milky Way Elementary School](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#milky-way-elementary-school). 
  - She was classmates with [Daisy](/the-library/en/sma/characters/daisy) during grades 5 and 6.
  - Krishna is referred to as Olive, perhaps hinting to her past name being Olive, but the tags still tag Krishna.[^17][^18]

### Junior High School

- Krishna went to [Sakura Junior High](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#sakura-junior-high). 
  - She was classmates with [Cyrus](/the-library/en/sma/characters/cyrus) and [Aimee](/the-library/en/sma/characters/aimee). She was classmates with [Samiel](/the-library/en/sma/characters/samiel) during grades 1 and 2.[^16] She was roommates with Aimee during grades 1 and 2. 
- In 1st grade, Krishna was once discovered by Aimee to be a sorcerer, but she deleted Aimee's memories.[^6]
- Krishna and Aimee do not seem to be on friendly terms.[^9]
- Something happened in grade 2, causing Krishna to no longer be Aimee's roommate.[^16]

## Trivia

- She lives in the same dorm room as Ophelia.
  - They live in an accessible dorm room. Krishna states that the main reason is that normal dorm rooms have bunk beds, which are difficult for her to climb. [^1]
  - Their dorm room has a mini fridge. 
- Her third language is Russian.[^13]
- She is the class treasurer in class.[^5]
- Her price of magic is that she would lose touch with her sense of self. The most prominent feature is numbed emotions, meaning she only feels emotions over a certain intensity.[^11]
- She senses magic by the sense of touch. She feels cold when feeling the trace of magic.[^7]
- Her [marking of magic](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/sorcerer/#markings-of-magic) is in her right eye, and she conceals it using her right hearing aid, her hair clip, and an anklet.[^8]
- Krishna has several physical disabilities. 
  - Named ones include achromatopsia[^1], hearing impairment[^2], loss of sense of smell and taste[^1], and an injury on her right wrist[^10]. 
  - She also has issues with chronic pain somewhere in her legs, and uses a cane on most days or a wheelchair on bad days.[^4] She is capable of walking small distances without a cane, but cannot stand for too long.[^11]
  - She uses hearing aids. 
  - Krishna mentions being blind in one eye. It is unclear if the marking of magic in her right eye affects her vision, or if this is a lie to justify her eyepatch. 
- Krishna's right eye might have powers connected to memory.[^13]
- Currently, the only mental disorder that Krishna is explicitly said to have is OSDD-1b.[^15]
  - There are also hints to Krishna having some condition related to speech.[^18][^7]
- Krishna's alters that we know of include Space, Shadow, Ink, and Flame.[^14]
  - Space is the host. Space also seems to have internalized ableism and tendencies to push the body too hard. 
  - Shadow seems to be a protector and emergency fronter. Shadow refers to Space with the nickname Star. Shadow uses they/them pronouns. The difference between Shadow and Space fronting is subtle, but people close to Krishna sometimes notice that Krishna is colder than usual.[^7][^18]
  - Ink is a little, and might be a holder for physical pain. Ink also refers to Ophelia as Ophie. 
  - Flame is only mentioned, but Flame might be a protector. 
- Krishna goes to therapy.[^15]
- Krishna skips meals sometimes.[^1][^10]
- Krishna is averse to touch. 
- It is implied that the hair clip Krishna wears is a gift from Daisy in the past.[^12]
