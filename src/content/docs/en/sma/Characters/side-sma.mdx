This is a list of side characters in the universe of [Summermist Academy](/en/sma/sma). 

## Basil

**Basil** (羽萍) is the younger brother of Daisy. 

Basil is currently in grade 3 of junior high school.

Basil appears in [Good Night](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/814156136562507776/good-night).

Basil is mentioned in [Flower Language](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/785508008083013632/flower-language). 

## Ophelia's grandmother 

We currently do not have a name for Ophelia's grandmother. 

Ophelia's grandmother is a sorcerer, specifically a spirit sorcerer. Her price of magic is losing touch with humanity, which means it is hard for her to feel bodily needs and other aspects of humanity. She is in Knot For Bookworms, and it is also why the book club often holds meetings at their house. 

Ophelia's grandmother wears an eyepatch on her left eye.

Ophelia's grandmother appears in [Meetings](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/770011538844205056/meetings) and [Shattered Illusions](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/790933167280390144/shattered-illusions). 

## Michael

**Michael** (璟) is the younger brother of Samiel. 

Michael uses a wheelchair and relies on Samiel's care. 

Michael appears in [Coming Home](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/797032016832004096/coming-home). 

Michael is mentioned in [Extracurricular Activities](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/764657579255283712/extracurricular20activities).

## Aqua

**Aqua** (小青) is a member of team A Plus. 

Aqua can often keep his calm. 

Aqua appears in [Waiting For Other Players](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/762064570026278912/waiting-for-other-players) and [Filming Strategies](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/804326632927133696/filming-strategies).

## Maple

**Maple** (楓糖) is a member of team A Plus. 

Maple is an energetic girl. 

Maple appears in [Waiting For Other Players](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/762064570026278912/waiting-for-other-players) and [Filming Strategies](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/804326632927133696/filming-strategies).

Maple is mentioned in [Matching Usernames](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/796489422828863488/matching-usernames).

## Anaka

**Anaka** is a member of Knot For Bookworms. 

His English name is Noah, and he is an advanced water sorcerer. 

Anaka appears in [Meetings](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/770011538844205056/meetings) and [Shattered Illusions](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/790933167280390144/shattered-illusions). 

## Audrey

**Audrey** is the leader of Knot For Bookworms. 

She is responsible for greeting and testing newcomers to the book club. 

Audrey appears in [Shattered Illusions](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/790933167280390144/shattered-illusions).
