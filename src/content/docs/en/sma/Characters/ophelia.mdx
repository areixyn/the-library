**Ophelia Summers** (夏茵鵲) is a main character in [Summermist Academy](/en/sma/sma).

## Basic Information

- English Name: Ophelia Summers
- Chinese Name: 夏茵鵲
- Other Names: Lein (shasuti)
- Number: 4
- Birthday: 06.03 (Gemini)
- Gender: female (she/her)
- Sexuality: bisexual
- Height: 155
- Weight: average 
- Representative Color: blue
- Representative Item: cookie
- Club(s): Dessert Club, Spirits of the Air
- Hobbies: writing, baking
- Fashion Style: casual chic
- Magic: [fire sorcerer](/en/sma/worldbuilding/sorcerer)

## Appearance

Ophelia has brown hair tied in a long ponytail that reaches her waist. She has M-shaped bangs with short sideburns. She has a light blue bow on her ponytail. Her eyes are light blue.

In [uniform](/en/sma/worldbuilding/summermist-academy/#uniform), Ophelia wears an oversized cardigan vest — purple with white trims — over her uniform. She wears a long sleeved uniform. Her tie is a ribbon bow tie. She wears a skirt with white knee high socks. There are two blue stripes on the top of her socks. She wears dark blue loafers.

Ophelia's fashion style is casual chic. Her casual outfit is a fake two piece shirt that looks like a cream colored shirt with the collar and cuffs of a white blouse with blue stripes. The top has three quarter sleeves. She wears a light blue dress that reaches her calves with white above ankle socks and the same loafers as her school outfit. 

## Past

### Elementary School 

- Ophelia went to [Sunset Elementary School](/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#sunset-elementary-school). 
  - She was classmates with [Aimee](/en/sma/characters/aimee) during grades 5 and 6.[^11]
- [Krishna](/en/sma/characters/krishna) knows Ophelia from a past event, although currently it is unknown what this event is. Ophelia does not seem to know about this. 
  - Krishna appears to know a lot about Ophelia's magic, including the fact that she is a sorcerer[^6] and her [price of magic](/en/sma/worldbuilding/magic-sma/#price-of-magic)[^8]. 
  - The two had specific ways for comforting each other. Krishna would pat Ophelia's shoulder[^6], while Ophelia would hold Krishna's hands[^7].
  - In *Shattered Illusions*[^7], Ophelia has an impression that Krishna cannot speak during her implied panic attack / alter switch.
  - In *First Meeting*[^5], Krishna recognizes and calls out Ophelia's name when they first meet in high school, but Ophelia does not recognize her. 

### Junior High School 

- Ophelia went to [Snowflake Junior High](/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#snowflake-junior-high). 
  - She was classmates with [Arashi](/en/sma/characters/arashi) during 3rd grade.

### Other

- Ophelia sometimes has nightmares. 
  - One of the recurring nightmares is about escaping a building that is on fire.[^13]
  - She seems to avoid thinking about the nightmares too much, and often doesn't remember about the contents of the dream. 
  - She often drinks warm milk to cope after nightmares. 
  - In *Warm Milk*[^4], Ophelia mentions a girl that may be related to the nightmares. She mentions that the girl pats her shoulders to comfort her, and that she doesn't like thinking about her. 

## Trivia

- She lives in the same dorm room as Krishna. 
  - They live in an accessible room, and while Krishna has disabilities that explain why she does, it is unknown why Ophelia also got assigned to this room.[^5]
  - Their dorm room has a mini fridge. 
- Her third language is Korean.
- She is the vice class president in class.
- She lives with her grandmother and grandfather. As of now, we have not heard about Ophelia's father or mother. 
  - Her grandparents are retired, and they are a low income family.[^10]
- Her price of magic is that she is unable to express anger.[^8]
- Her [marking of magic](/en/sma/worldbuilding/sorcerer/#markings-of-magic) is on her right calf, and she conceals it using a necklace.[^2] 
  - The necklace was given to her by her grandmother.[^3]
- Ophelia refers to Aimee using the nickname Mimi. 
- Ophelia's grandmother is also a sorcerer, specifically a spirit sorcerer. 
  - They are both in Knot For Bookworms, and it is also why the book club often holds meetings at their house.[^1] 
- Ophelia has a part time job.[^9]
- Ophelia is a fan of QU33NBIRD.[^12] 
  - She is more of a casual fan. 
  - She was pulled into the fandom by Aimee. They watch the newest music videos together.
  - It is hinted that Ophelia's favorite member is Roselynn.
