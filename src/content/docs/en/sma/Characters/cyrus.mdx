**Cyrus Bluestone** (藍梓彥) is a main character in [Summermist Academy](/the-library/en/sma/sma).

- English Name: Cyrus Bluestone
- Chinese Name: 藍梓彥
- Other Names: Akaoni (online name)

- Fashion Style: sporty
- Magic: none

## Appearance

Cyrus has short black hair. His bangs are parted at the left. His eyes are light brown. His skin is darker than most of the others.

In [uniform](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/summermist-academy/#uniform), Cyrus wears a short sleeved uniform. His tie is a short necktie. He has a black fanny pack that has orange zippers over his right shoulder. He wears black sport wristbands with two orange stripes. He wears shorts along with white above ankle socks and black and orange sneakers.

Cyrus's fashion style is sporty. His casual outfit is a white T shirt with orange sleeves and trim. He wears the same fanny pack and wristbands as his school outfit. He wears black pants with a white strip along the sides. He wears white above ankle socks with orange and white sneakers. 

## Past

### Elementary School

- Cyrus went to [Cloudtop Elementary School](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#cloudtop-elementary-school) during grades 1 to 3.
  - He was friends with [Samiel](/the-library/en/sma/characters/samiel), although the two are not in the same grade.[^4][^5]
- Cyrus went to [Sunset Elementary School](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#sunset-elementary-school) during grades 4 to 6.[^9]
  - He was classmates with [Aimee](/the-library/en/sma/characters/aimee) during grade 4.[^8]

### Junior High School 

- Cyrus went to [Sakura Junior High](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#sakura-junior-high). 
  - He was classmates with [Krishna](/the-library/en/sma/characters/krishna) and Aimee. He was classmates with Samiel during grades 1 and 2.[^9] 
- Cyrus and Aimee were dating during grades 1 and 2 of junior high school.
  - During this, they both left CL Clan and Team A Plus.[^3]

## Trivia

- His third language is Russian. 
  - This was not picked by him, but instead assigned because he forgot to pick the course.[^6]
- He is the chief of PE in class.
- Cyrus's parents sell luwei in a night market.[^3] He also has an older sister. 
- Cyrus is a member of Team A Plus in [CL Clan](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/duel-before-dusk/#cl-clan).
  - He left once, but is back now.
- Cyrus's in game (Duel Before Dawn) character is a girl that has blonde hair and blue eyes. She has tanned skin and tan lines, and she wears a red sports bra, black shorts, and a white jacket tied to her waist.[^2]
- Cyrus is implied to be a student athlete and was enrolled in Summermist Academy due to this.[^1]
