**Aimee Miller** (謝文扇) is a main character in [Summermist Academy](/en/sma/sma).

## Basic Information

- English Name: Aimee Miller
- Chinese Name: 謝文扇
- Other Names: Flisia (online name)
- Number: 2
- Birthday: 05.26 (Gemini)
- Gender: female (she/her)
- Sexuality: pansexual
- Height: 160
- Weight: average 
- Representative Color: yellow
- Representative Item: freesia
- Club(s): Dessert Club, Dancing Club
- Hobbies: dancing, gaming, idol chasing
- Fashion Style: k-fashion
- Magic: [perceiver](/en/sma/worldbuilding/perceiver)

## Appearance

Aimee has wavy strawberry blonde hair that reaches her shoulders. Her hair is pink at the ends. She is seen having different hairstyles. Her bangs are mostly swept to the right side, leaving only a strand of hair on her left. Her eyes are amber. Her skin is paler and closer to white people than most of the others.

In [uniform](/en/sma/worldbuilding/summermist-academy/#uniform), Aimee wears a cropped cardigan vest — purple with white trims — over her uniform. She wears a short sleeved uniform. She ties her hair into a half up braided hairstyle while wearing a yellow beret with freesias on the brim. On her left ear, she wears an ear cuff shaped like a butterfly wing with a chain attached to an earring. Her tie is a ribbon bow tie. On her right hand is a bracelet with a freesia charm. She wears a skirt with black knee high socks. On the right side of her skirt is a gold belt chain with small butterfly charms. She wears light brown loafers with freesia charms.

Aimee's fashion style is k-fashion. Her casual outfit is a yellow off shoulder crop top with short sleeves. She ties her hair into a half up high ponytail with a hair tie that has a yellow bow. She wears yellow butterfly shaped earrings and a black choker with a heart charm. She wears the same bracelet as her school outfit. She wears a black leather skirt with a black belt and silver belt chains. She wears black mid calf boots with yellow freesia charms. 

## Past

### Elementary School 

- Aimee went to [Sunset Elementary School](/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#sunset-elementary-school). 
  - She was classmates with [Cyrus](/en/sma/characters/cyrus) during grade 4.[^12]
  - She was classmates with [Ophelia](/en/sma/characters/ophelia) during grades 5 and 6.[^8]

### Junior High School 

- Aimee went to [Sakura Junior High](/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#sakura-junior-high). 
  - She was classmates with [Krishna](/en/sma/characters/krishna) and Cyrus. She was classmates with [Samiel](/en/sma/characters/samiel) during grades 1 and 2.[^10] She was roommates with Krishna during grades 1 and 2.
- In 1st grade, Aimee once discovered her to be a [sorcerer](/en/sma/worldbuilding/sorcerer), but she deleted Aimee's memories.[^4]
- Aimee and Krishna do not seem to be on friendly terms.[^5]
- Cyrus and Aimee were dating during grades 1 and 2 of junior high school.
  - During this, they both left CL Clan.[^11]
- Something happened in grade 2, causing Krishna to no longer be Aimee's roommate.[^10]

## Trivia

- Her third language is Korean.
- She is the English Teacher's Assistant in class.
- Aimee's parents work at a flower shop.[^7]
  - This also causes Aimee to know a lot about flower language. 
  - Her family is hinted to be rich, such as Aimee being willing to buy more expensive drinks[^3]. 
- She senses magic by smell.[^2]
  - Water magic smells like hyacinths. 
  - Fire magic smells like roses. 
  - Wind magic smells like jasmine. 
  - Earth magic smells like mint. 
  - Spirit magic smells like lavender.[^6]
- Aimee was once a member of Team A Plus in CL Clan.
- Aimee refers to Ophelia using the nickname Lili. 
- Aimee has an Instagram account that has quite some followers. She posts pictures with stickers and has a deliberate aesthetic for her posts.[^1]
- Freesias are Aimee's favorite flowers.[^7]
- Aimee is a fan of QU33NBIRD.[^9] 
  - She is an avid fan, keeping track of the band's activities.
  - She pulled Ophelia into the fandom. They watch the newest music videos together.
  - It is hinted that Aimee's favorite member is Scarlett.
