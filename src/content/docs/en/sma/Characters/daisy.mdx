**Daisy Aurora** (黎羽田) is a main character in [Summermist Academy](/the-library/en/sma/sma).

## Basic Information

- English Name: Daisy Aurora
- Chinese Name: 黎羽田
- Number: 6
- Birthday: 04.12 (Aries)
- Gender: female (she/her)
- Sexuality: homosexual 
- Height: 153
- Weight: underweight 
- Representative Color: purple
- Representative Item: bow
- Club(s): Spread Love Club
- Hobbies: DIY
- Fashion Style: cottagecore
- Magic: none

## Appearance

Daisy has ginger hair in a bob cut. She has blunt bangs and a short ahoge. On her left side, She has a light purple bow in her hair. Her eyes are purple.

In [uniform](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/summermist-academy/#uniform), Daisy wears the long sleeved uniform. Her tie is a bow tie. She wears a skirt with white over the knee socks. She has a ball shaped purple penguin plushie on her belt. Her left sock is adorned with purple ribbons and a bow on the side. She wears purple mary jane shoes.

Daisy's fashion style is cottagecore. Her casual outfit is a white square collar tulle dress that reaches her thighs with a purple bow in the center of the collar. She wears a purple jacket over her dress. She wears white over the knee socks, with a light purple ribbon as a garter on the left side. She wears purple mary jane shoes with bows. 

## Past

### Elementary School

- Daisy went to [Milky Way Elementary School](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#milky-way-elementary-school). 
  - She was classmates with [Arashi](/the-library/en/sma/characters/arashi) during grades 3 and 4. 
  - She was classmates with [Krishna](/the-library/en/sma/characters/krishna) during grades 5 and 6.[^8][^9]
  - Krishna is referred to as Olive, perhaps hinting to her past name being Olive, but the tags still tag Krishna.[^8][^9]
- In 3rd grade, Arashi told Daisy about her magic.[^6]

### Junior High School

- Daisy went to [Snowflake Junior High](/the-library/en/sma/worldbuilding/list-of-schools-sma/#snowflake-junior-high). 
  - She was classmates with Arashi during junior high school, though not during 3rd grade.[^4][^5]
  - She was friends with [Ophelia](/the-library/en/sma/characters/ophelia), although the two are not in the same class.[^5]

## Trivia

- She lives in the same dorm room as Arashi.
  - They've decorated their room with a cozy corner that features a rug, pillows, and bean bags.[^2]
  - They share a laundry basket.[^3]
- Her third language is Japanese.
- Daisy is the class secretary in the class.[^1]
- She has a younger brother named [Basil](/the-library/en/sma/characters/side-sma/#basil), younger than her by a year.[^7]
- Daisy likes small animals a lot.
- Daisy has a purple pen with white polka dots and a tiny ribbon charm on top. It is implied to be a gift from Krishna in the past.[^1]
- Daisy is bad at history.[^7]
