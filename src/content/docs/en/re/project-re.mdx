**Project RE** is a universe in Noxeris.

What should you do when your world gets torn apart?

In an icy wasteland, four people live together, each with their own secrets. Life seems idle when there's no one else but them, but can they survive all the shadows of the past?

## Trivia

- Project RE is mostly written by Areixyn, but Y is also a co author. 
- The universe code for Project RE is re.
