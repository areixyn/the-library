## Appearance

Kemys has short honey brown hair in a pixie cut. She has short choppy bangs and thin, long sideburns that reach her shoulders. She wears a yellow headset with a clear visor, a device called ANCU-3. The headset is black on certain parts like the buttons and has a pair of antennas on the headband and bee wings on the sides of the headset. She has yellow eyes. 

She wears a black high collar vest zipped all the way to the top. On the collar are two yellow stripes, one thick and one thin, and the zipper pull is shaped like a needle. The vest has a pocket with a yellow zipper and three small yellow pockets on her right side and a big orangish yellow pocket on the left. The zipper pull on the pocket is a silver hollow circle, and it is connected with a thin chain to the middle one of the three small pockets. The bottom of the vest is shaped like two pairs of bee wings, with the front flaps shorter and the back ones longer, reaching her knees. The inside of the vest is an orangish yellow. Underneath she wears a black top with the right arm cut off, leaving her prosthetic arm bare. On her right arm is an orangish yellow armband with a zipper pocket, and the zipper pull is shaped like a needle. On her left arm are two yellow arm bands, and the bottom one has a silver chain. She wears a brown fingerless glove lined with white fur on her left hand. She has a prosthetic right forearm, and it is mostly white, except for the joints which are black. She wears a yellow belt over her vest, and a device is attached to the belt on the left side. The device has a screen and a black shell with bits of gold. It has two white buttons on top and a small chain on the bottom left corner. She wears black cargo shorts with two yellow stripes at the bottom, one thick and one thin. On the right side of the pants is a brown pocket with a yellow belt attached. The end of the belt is a needle shaped pendant. She wears black leggings underneath. She wears brown mid calf combat boots lined with white fur.

### Spring Sunshine Children's Home

Kemys and Sakina were in Spring Sunshine Children's Home in the past. It is unclear how long this period was, our only clue being that they were both in the children's home when Kemys was 10.[^4] 

During this period, the two were close friends, and often did everything together. In particular, they often stay in the library and read together.[^5] It is also implied that the other children in the children's home were not close with the two, and that they were each other's only friend.[^4][^5] The two also used to have code signs, one being a sign for checking on the other[^1]. 

Notably, Kemys's and Sakina's personalities seem different back then. Kemys seems more vibrant and cheerful. On the contrary, Sakina seems more quiet, rarely signing or expressing emotions.[^4] 

Kemys went to a deaf school during this period.[^4]

### In Light Of Shadows Institute

Kemys and Akinari were both in the Warfare Department of LOS.[^8] Although it is hinted that the two used to be close at some point, they currently are in a tense relationship. She is wary of Akinari and deems Akinari “not deserving of praying with her”[^2]. 

### Other

- Kemys's family has a taja, which hints that her whole family is religious[^3]. 
- Kemys once talked about a person who used to give her tea and gave her advice about what to do when she couldn't sleep[^6]. 

## Trivia

- Kemys is deaf. She has a device called ANCU-3, which is a pair of headphones with visors, and she usually uses it to communicate. She knows sign language, but rarely uses it. 
  - ANCU-3 can generate captions on the visor, allowing Kemys to see what people say. It also has a speaker that allows Kemys to communicate using text-to-speech, although currently it is unclear how Kemys inputs text to the machine[^2].
- Kemys likes yellow because she likes bees[^1].
- Kemys is a devout follower of Choreya, and participates in the religion's rituals[^2].
- Kemys cares a lot about rules and orders, preferring to follow them very strictly.[^7]
