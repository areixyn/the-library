**Akinari** (秋晴) is a main character in Project RE.

## Basic Information

- English Name: Akinari
- Chinese Name: 秋晴
- Other Names: <Spoiler>Kokatsu Kemusaki</Spoiler>
- Gender: N/A (none; accepts nickname "En")
- Birthday: 05/13 (Taurus) <Spoiler>This is a fake birthday. Akinari does not have a birthday.[^3]</Spoiler>
- Height: 170
- Weight: average
- Representative Color: brown
- Representative Item: wilted cherry blossom

## Appearance

<Spoiler>In human form,</Spoiler> Akinari has long hair that reaches Akinari's waist. Akinari's hair has a gradient that is black on top and brown at the ends. Akinari has long m-shaped bangs, and the middle part of En's bangs is parted slightly like a cherry blossom petal. Akinari has a Chinese hair pin with a ring; the ring part is light brown and shaped like tree branches with a white cherry blossom petal between, and the pin is brown with a string of three white cherry blossom petals on the end. Akinari has a half up hairstyle with the pin. Akinari's eyes are dark brown, and the pupils are shaped like a 6 pointed star (pointier than a hexagram). Akinari's eyes are drawn without highlights. 

Akinari wears an off shoulder cape that is brown and turns light brown at the edges. The cape has a dark brown lapel around the top edge, and on the right side is a white cherry blossom petal that holds the cape together. From the petal comes a dark brown string that wraps around the cape. The bottom edge of the cape is torn, and eight white petals are scattered across the edge, with four visible from the front. Under the cape Akinari wears a black turtleneck sleeveless top. Akinari wears a choker that is a light brown band with a white petal pendant. A section of Akinari's right arm is wrapped tightly with brown bandages. Akinari wears black fingerless gloves. Akinari wears two light brown belts, one at the waist and one loosely draped under the first one. A brown belt bag is attached to the belts on the right side, and on the bottom belt three spikes hang at the left side. Akinari wears dark brown shorts. At the right side of the shorts is a slit with two layers in the slit, the two layers being brown and light brown. At the top of the slit is a white petal. Akinari wears black leggings underneath. A small section of Akinari's left leg is wrapped tightly with light brown bandages, which functions like a thigh band. Akinari wears dark brown knee high combat boots. On both boots are light brown boot holsters, with the left one having two straps and the right having one. 

## Past

This section contains **major** spoilers for the story.

### As Kokatsu

- Akinari became friends with Masaki, who is Chizakura. Masaki often visits Akinari during sunset, which is when her work ends and Akinari's begins. 
- Akinari once claims that Akinari and Masaki are technically twins separated in childhood, and they don't know much about each other's early lives. This could be referring to how Chizakura and Kokatsu are said to be twin gods in mythology, but Masaki and Akinari likely grew up in different places (one being in Lessaku Realm, the other being in Choreya Realm).[^3]

### In Light Of Shadows Institute

Akinari and Kemys were both in the Warfare Department of LOS.[^6] Although it is hinted that the two used to be close at some point, they currently are in a tense relationship. 

## Trivia

- Akinari can sign, and often uses sign to communicate with Sakina and Kemys. Akinari sometimes signs and speaks simultaneously, although it's unclear if this affects sign or spoken language. 
- Akinari refers to Masaki using the nickname Saki.
  - The nickname comes from Akinari associating Masaki with cherry blossoms in bloom.[^4]
- Akinari's magic is described as unnoticeable. The effect this has is that one might feel as if during a moment they were distracted, Akinari has finished casting spells, potentially leading to confusion.[^1]
- Akinari sleeps during the afternoon. 
- Akinari prefers not to use any labels in terms of gender[^2]. Technically this might mean that Akinari is unlabeled, although this is never explicitly stated.
- Akinari chose May 13th as Akinari's birthday because it is the day the last cherry blossoms wilt away[^3].
- Akinari used to make meals with magic, but recently picked up cooking due to having too much time.[^5]
