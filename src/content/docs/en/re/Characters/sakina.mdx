**Sakina** (晴雅) is a main character in [Project RE](/the-library/en/re/project-re).

## Appearance

Sakina has short hair reaching their shoulders that's white on her left side and black on her right side. They have a jellyfish haircut and blunt bangs. Their sideburns are the same length as the top of their jellyfish haircut. They have eyes that are light blue on the top half and light red on the bottom. 

They wear a light blue scarf that has ends with fur clouds and a gradient. One of the ends, the one they have in front, has a light blue to white gradient while the other has a light blue to blue gradient. They wear a blue cape that has a collar that looks like a sailor shirt with a sailor tie. The collar is white while the tie is light blue. The ends of the tie look like butterfly wings, and on the left side the ends are white and the right side black. The cape is lined with white fur that looks like clouds. Under the cape, they wear a dark blue double breasted suit jacket with blue cuffs. The jacket is cropped and has half sleeves. Underneath the jacket they wear a white long sleeved top with finger loops, making the cuffs look like a tekkou. On their left hand is a blue and light blue butterfly shaped wrist cuff, and their right hand is loosely wrapped with light red ribbons. On the right of their waist is a white fluffy ball with a light red cherry blossom in the center and a blue string that comes out of it and functions like a belt chain. They wear dark blue dress shorts and white leggings underneath. On their left leg is a blue and light blue butterfly shaped thigh garter. They wear dark blue ankle boots, and the right boot is loosely wrapped with light red ribbons.

## Past

### In Light Of Shadows Institute

Sakina and [Masaki](/the-library/en/re/characters/masaki) were both in the Medical Department of [LOS](/the-library/en/re/worldbuilding/light-of-shadows-institute). Sakina was born in the lab, with Masaki's and [Akinari](/the-library/en/re/characters/akinari)'s magic in their body. This causes them to be able to use both kinds of magic, such as healing better than others, but also makes them susceptible to the magic going out of control.[^8] 

Masaki knows about Sakina's condition, and knows how to heal Sakina when they fall ill. It is implied that this has happened a lot in the past.[^5]

### Spring Sunshine Children's Home

Sakina and [Kemys](/the-library/en/re/characters/kemys) were in [Spring Sunshine Children's Home](/the-library/en/re/worldbuilding/inkeloss/#spring-sunshine-childrens-home) in the past. It is unclear how long this period was, our only clue being that they were both in the children's home when Kemys was 10.[^4] 

During this period, the two were close friends, and often did everything together. In particular, they often stay in the library and read together.[^3] It is also implied that the other children in the children's home were not close with the two, and that they were each other's only friend.[^3][^4] The two also used to have code signs, one being a sign for checking on the other[^1]. 

Notably, Sakina's and Kemys's personalities seem different back then. Sakina seems more quiet, rarely signing or expressing emotions. On the contrary, Kemys seems more vibrant and cheerful.[^4] Sakina finds Kemys to be a comforting presence back then, seeing Kemys as "warm" and "like a bright yellow sun lighting her up."[^1]

### Other

- Sakina was once referred to as "Lynne" in a nightmare[^1]. It is unsure whether this is their past name or something else. 
- Sakina once attended a school that had strict rules for clothing[^1].
- Sakina's parents seem to be followers of [Lessaku](/the-library/en/re/worldbuilding/lessaku)[^2]. 

## Trivia

- Sakina is mute. They use sign language and occasionally writing to communicate.
  - Sakina seems to have limited vocabulary in sign language. 
- Sakina's affinity for the sky stems from the first time they saw the sky[^1]. 
- Before remembering their favorite color, Sakina answered pink when asked about their favorite color. 
- Sakina is a follower of [Lessaku](/the-library/en/re/worldbuilding/lessaku), and participates in the religion's rituals. 
- Sakina has religious trauma. 
  - Sakina is very disturbed when they miss out on bailis, one of Lessaku's rituals[^2]. She also mentions praying for Chizakura's forgiveness about missing baili.
  - Sakina had been taught that being transgender was bad in Chizakura's eyes. She thought that [Masaki](/the-library/en/re/characters/masaki) would be upset at her for being agender<Spoiler> when learning that Masaki is Chizakura</Spoiler>, and this lead to her trying to pretend to be cisgender.[^7]
- Sakina seems to be sick often, and experiences sickness more severely than average. They are also capable of clearly describing their symptoms.[^5] <Spoiler>This is likely due to her experiences in the lab.</Spoiler>
- <Spoiler>When Sakina uses Chizakura magic, the light red part of their eyes turns pink, and when they use Kokatsu magic it turns orange.</Spoiler>
