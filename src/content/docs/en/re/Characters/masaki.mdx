**Masaki** (雅紀) is a main character in Project RE.

## Basic Information

- English Name: Masaki
- Chinese Name: 雅紀
- Other Names: <Spoiler>Chizakura Arashiko</Spoiler>
- Gender: female (she/her)
- Birthday: <Spoiler>Masaki does not have a birthday. She makes up one every time she's asked.[^5]</Spoiler>
- Height: 148
- Weight: average
- Representative Color: pink
- Representative Item: cherry blossom

## Appearance

  src={masNormal}
  alt="Masaki's normal outfit."
  caption="Masaki's normal outfit."

<Spoiler>In human form,</Spoiler> Masaki has short, curly hair. Her hair has a gradient that is white on top and light pink at the ends. She has short m-shaped bangs, and the middle part of her bangs is parted slightly like a cherry blossom petal. She has two longer braids coming out from behind her ears, and the braids reach her shoulders. She has a heart shaped ahoge. Her eyes are pink, and her pupils are shaped like a cherry blossom petal. 

She wears a kimono-like top, with the right half light pink and left half pink. The juban underneath is muted brown on the right side and white on the other. The kimono sleeves are short and lined with white lace. The right sleeve has a pink cherry blossom icon on the side. On her left hand is a white wrist cuff that is shaped like feathers on a wing and has a pink band in the middle securing the wrist band. She wears a muted brown obi with a pink obijime. The obidome is completely pink, and it is a mizuhiki cherry blossom with a pink gem in the middle and a short tassel. She wears a layered skirt, with the outer layer being light pink and covering the right half of the skirt and the inner layer being pink and also longer, covering the left half. The inner layer and the outer layer overlap a little. On top of the skirt, there is a wing shaped piece of fabric on her right. Next to the light pink part of the skirt is a short band of dark pink with five white diamonds from top to bottom. On her right leg is a muted brown thigh garter that has a band of white rope through the middle. She wears mid calf white socks that look like bird wings on the cuff. On the sides of the socks are outlines of a cherry blossom. She wears a pair of pokkuri geta, with the wooden part a muted brown and the ropes dark pink. 

## Past

This section contains **major** spoilers for the story.
</Aside>

### As Chizakura

- Masaki became friends with Akinari, who is Kokatsu. She often visits Akinari during sunset, which is when her work ends and Akinari's begins. 

### In Light Of Shadow Institute

Masaki and Sakina were both in the Medical Department of LOS. Sakina was born in the lab, with Masaki's and Akinari's magic in their body. This causes them to be able to use both kinds of magic, such as healing better than others, but also makes them susceptible to the magic going out of control.[^4] 

Masaki knows about Sakina's condition, and knows how to heal Sakina when they fall ill. It is implied that this has happened a lot in the past.[^3]

## Trivia

- Masaki understands sign language, but usually uses spoken language with Sakina and Kemys. 
- Masaki gives nicknames to most people she knows, and sometimes items, too. 
  - Current known nicknames include: 
  - Nana for Sakina
  - Mimi for Kemys
  - Mumu for Akinari
  - Ruru for Stanwatt
  - Tree Branch is Masaki's staff, although it is currently unsure if this is a nickname or its actual name.
- When Masaki uses magic, her hands or staff glows a white light. 
- Masaki sometimes works during the afternoon. This work includes stamping on documents and approving certain paperwork. <Spoiler>This work is later revealed to be the duties of being Chizakura.</Spoiler>
