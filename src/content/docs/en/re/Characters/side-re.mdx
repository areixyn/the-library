This is a list of side characters in [Project RE](/the-library/en/re/project-re). 

## Stanwatt

**Stanwatt Ron Smonlin** is [Masaki](the-library/en/re/characters/masaki)'s assistant. 

<Spoiler>Stanwatt doesn't seem to be a [kafuku](the-library/en/re/worldbuilding/lessaku).</Spoiler>

Stanwatt appears in [A Visitor](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/805729588450918400/a-visitor).

## Yoi

**Yoi** is [Akinari](the-library/en/re/characters/akinari)'s assistant. 

Yoi refers to Akinari with the nickname Batsu. 

Yoi appears in [New Books](https://areixyn-en.tumblr.com/post/822225022815993856/new-books).
