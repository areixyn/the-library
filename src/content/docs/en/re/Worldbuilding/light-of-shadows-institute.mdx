Light Of Shadows Institute, or LOS for short, is a secret government institute in Inkeloss. The motto of LOS is “We search for the brightest light in the darkest shadows.”[^2]

LOS conducts research on magic and experiments on magic users.[^1] The project on magic research is called Project RE, the motto of which is “Realize, Reharness, Repurpose.”[^2]

## Departments

Currently, we only know of two departments: the Medical Department and the Warfare Department.
