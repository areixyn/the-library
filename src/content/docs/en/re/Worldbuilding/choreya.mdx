**Choreya** is a fictional religion in Project RE. 

## Basic Information

Choreya believers worship Kokatsu, the god of war. As the public mostly follow Lessaku, Choreya followers are often seen as dangerous, selfish, or insane, among others.[^2] However, the principles of Choreya are actually centered on justice and the idea of doing the right thing, even if that means breaking the peace.[^3] According to Kemys, Kokatsu only punishes those who have done wrong. 

## Myths

Currently, we only know a fraction about the myth of the origins of the two gods.[^4] Below is a summary of the myth, but do note that it was written mostly through the lenses of Lessaku. 

Chizakura and Kokatsu were at first gods of day and night, and both had the duty of keeping peace among humans. Kokatsu was also originally known as Yozakura. Under their rule, humans did not know conflict and would live peacefully. However, humans neglected and ignored the god of night (Yozakura) for a long time, leading to them eventually deciding to bring destruction and war upon humans by manipulating their emotions and causing people to fight against each other. It is at this point that they abandoned the name Yozakura, instead becoming Kokatsu, the god of war. Eventually Chizakura teaches humans how to stay kind and cooperate, and also uses a flood to cleanse the world of thoughts of conflict, leading to the end of the wars. Despite Chizakura’s attempt to convince Kokatsu to return to before (keeping peace), Kokatsu refuses and curses humans to have emotions that might lead to them being in conflict again.

## Trivia

- The symbol for Choreya is a hollow circle, which symbolizes the new moon during Kokatsu’s destruction of the world.[^7]
- There are three main rules / principles of Choreya.[^8]
  - 1. Fight against yourself. 
  - 2. Fight against imbalance. 
  - 3. Fight against silence. 
- One of Choreya’s rituals is night prayers.[^1]
  - The exact time of prayer seems dependent on the position of the moon. 
  - Before praying, one should cleanse the mind and body by washing the hands, then face, mouth, ears, and the hands again, and then breathe in deeply, then out, and count to nine.
  - While praying, one will hold a brown candle in front of their chest and recite the scriptures. Then, they will kneel down, holding the candle up above their head, and recite the last part of the scriptures. They can then blow out the candle and stand up.
- Choreya does not have temples, but some families have a shrine called taja.[^2]
- The religious text of Choreya is the Shiki.[^5]
- Kokatsu only has one assistant called the yachoshi, or yacho for short.[^6]
- The place Kokatsu lives is called Soshiya.[^4]
- Many magic users follow Choreya, perhaps because Lessaku frames magic as a curse from Kokatsu and thus holds negative views on magic.
