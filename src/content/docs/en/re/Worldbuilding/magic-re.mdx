Magic exists in the universe of [Project RE](/the-library/en/re/project-re). It is based on imagination, and anything can happen as long as a magic user can imagine it and has enough power.[^1]

## Humans

Humans who can use masic are called magic users. Most countries seem to dislike magic users, and their rights are often not protected.[^2] The reason is currently unknown. 

## Magical Beings

For beings created from magic <Spoiler>such as [gods](/the-library/en/re/worldbuilding/gods)</Spoiler>, other than bringing imagination to reality, magic also makes them behave closer to how the majority of humans think they are like. Although this usually does not affect them too much, under extreme circumstances (such as repressing the magic too long), the magic can overpower the individual and make them behave exactly like whatever humans believe they are.[^3]
