**Inkeloss** is a fictional country in Project RE where Sakina and Kemys were born. 

## About Inkeloss

Inkeloss’s state religion seems to be Lessaku. It uses a different calendar, marked by “Ingmar” written in front of the year. There seems to be two official languages, Traditional Chinese and English.[^3]

As of now, Inkeloss is in a war.[^2]

## Spring Sunshine Children's Home

**Spring Sunshine Children's Home** is the children's home Sakina and Kemys went to. 

- There is a small library in the facility.[^1]
- Most of the kids go to a local elementary school, but depending on the child’s needs they might also be sent to other schools, such as a school for the deaf.[^1]
