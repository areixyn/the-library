The cast of Project RE currently live in an icy landscape. 

## The Igloo

They lived in an igloo temporarily. There are different rooms for everyone, and also a living room. There is also a (presumably magical) lamp that makes the igloo warm. 

## The Cottage

The cottage was built as a more permanent place for everyone to live.[^4] There are different rooms for everyone, and also a living room, a kitchen[^3], and a study[^2].

## Trivia

- There is a lates near their houses, built for Sakina to visit during Fridays.[^1]
